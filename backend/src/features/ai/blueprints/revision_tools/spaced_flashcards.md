## Interactive Mechanics
1. **3D Card Flip**: Smooth 3D perspective flip on click, Flip Button, or Spacebar.
2. **Keyboard Navigation**: Left Arrow (Prev), Right Arrow (Next), Space (Flip).
3. **Leitner Confidence Grading**: Easy / Medium / Hard rating pills to track spaced repetition mastery.
