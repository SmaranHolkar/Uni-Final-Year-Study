## Interactive Mechanics
1. Configurable 25m Focus / 5m Break / 15m Long Break intervals with smooth progress rings.
2. Built-in interactive study checklist where tasks can be ticked off.
3. Ambient sound generator (Lofi, Rain, White Noise) and audio chime notifications.
